# Advertiser portal (self-service external access)

The **portal** (`/portal`) is the **white-label** space you open to your advertisers:
each one sees **only their own campaigns**, their quantified reports, and can
**compose and submit** a new campaign on their own — which you approve before playback.

## Opening access for an advertiser

1. Page **Campaigns → Advertisers**, advertiser drawer, **Portal access** section.
2. **Invite** with an email: the account is created (Advertiser role) and the **initial
   password is displayed ONLY ONCE** — pass it on. If the email matches an existing
   account of the organization, it is attached to the advertiser (its internal roles are
   replaced).
3. An advertiser account that logs in is **automatically sent to the portal** —
   never to the CMS. Deleting the account (IAM) unlinks it from the advertiser.
4. The **"Demo account"** checkbox creates a demonstration access.

Isolation is total: an advertiser never sees the campaigns, rates or detailed occupancy
rates of others (availability is presented to them in aggregate:
**ok / tight / full**).

## What the advertiser can do

- **Track their campaigns**: statuses, approval badges, reports (SOV delivered,
  proven plays) and **PDF export**.
- **Create a campaign**: choice of **sites** on a map (sites incompatible with
  their industry category are greyed out), period, **SOV or Exclusive** (= 100 %),
  **availability and quote displayed live**, **Achievable SOV** banner where applicable.
  If your organization enables **"Per-screen selection (portal)"**, they can expand
  each site and tick screens individually.
- **Upload their creatives** (video/image) directly into the campaign — quotas
  adjustable per org (default 20 creatives / 1024 MB per file); every external creative
  is born **Pending** and will be reviewed.
- **Submit for approval**: the request goes into your approval queue. If
  **changes are requested**, they see the reviewer's reason, fix and **resubmit**.
- **Site lists**: they can save reusable site selections.
- **Notification bell**: they are notified of decisions (approved / rejected /
  changes requested) — and by **email** if you tick the decision notification.
- The portal exists in **5 languages** (selector at the top of the page).

## On the retailer side: nothing changes

The request arrives in **Approvals** (`/approvals`) with all the context: viewable
creatives, period, SOV, quote, occupancy of the requested screens (with the detail of
campaigns already in place). You approve, reject or request changes — actual
publication remains under your control.
